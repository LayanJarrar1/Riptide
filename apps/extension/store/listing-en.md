## Name

Riptide Download Manager

## Short description / summary (95 characters, same as the manifest)

Hands your downloads to Riptide, the free download manager, automatically or with one click.

## Long description

Riptide is a free, open-source download manager for Windows. This extension connects your browser to the Riptide desktop app so large downloads go to Riptide instead of the browser's own downloader.

Requires the Riptide desktop app (Windows 10 or 11), free from https://github.com/LayanJarrar1/Riptide/releases. The extension does nothing on its own.

What it does
• Automatic capture: when a download starts and matches your rules (file type, minimum size, site lists), it is handed to Riptide. If Riptide is not running or not installed, the browser simply keeps the download.
• "Download with Riptide" on the right-click menu for links, images, video and audio.
• "Download all links", "Download selected links" and "Download all images" open a checklist on the page, with type filters and search, so you can pick several files at once.
• A small "Download with Riptide" button appears when you hover a direct file link. You can turn it off.
• Hold Alt while clicking to let the browser download a file itself.
• Toolbar popup with the connection status, the last files you sent and a pause switch.
• English and Arabic (right-to-left), following your browser language or your choice.

What Riptide adds to your downloads
• Many connections per file, with the slowest parts split and shared while the download runs.
• Pause and resume that survives restarts and crashes, and refuses to mix two versions of a file.
• Checksum verification and safe file names.
• Downloads that need you to be signed in keep working: the cookies for that one address are passed to the app on your computer.

Privacy
The extension collects nothing. No analytics, no accounts, no remote code and no network access of its own: its only connection is the Native Messaging channel to the Riptide app on the same computer. Cookies are passed only for the address being downloaded, are sent only to that same site, and are deleted when the download completes.
Privacy policy: https://layanjarrar1.github.io/Riptide/privacy/
