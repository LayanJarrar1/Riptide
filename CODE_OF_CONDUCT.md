# Code of conduct

Riptide is a small project with contributors who speak different languages and come from
different places. We want working on it to be pleasant for everyone.

## Our standards

- Be kind and direct. Critique code and ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith. Ask before judging; most friction is a misunderstanding.
- Welcome newcomers. Point to the docs, explain the "why", and remember your own first pull
  request.
- Respect that people contribute in their own time. A review may take a few days.
- Arabic and English are both welcome in issues and pull requests. Write in whichever you are
  more comfortable with; maintainers will answer in kind.

The following are not acceptable anywhere in the project (issues, pull requests, discussions,
commit messages): harassment, insults or demeaning comments, discriminatory language or jokes,
publishing someone's private information, sexualised language or imagery, and persistent
disruption of discussions.

## Scope

This applies to every project space and to anyone representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour to the maintainers through GitHub (a private message to a
maintainer, or the private vulnerability-reporting form if nothing else is available). Reports are
handled confidentially. Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, and temporarily or
permanently block people who violate these standards. Maintainers who do not follow or enforce
this code may be removed from the project.
